# Phone layout: brief

[Up: mocks](../README.md)

**Problem.** Phone access serves the desktop layout. At 390 px there is no thread list to tap, so
jumping to a thread, switching workspace and finding an old one dead-end; the composer's options
popover splits into three columns; the Board's table clips; Needs you, History and the palette
are two-pane dialogs with key legends that close only with Esc. A blind phone-size round (M1)
scored Gram 4.75 Overall against 6.75 and 7.5 for two responsive reference apps.

**Who.** Someone away from their desk with a phone, checking on workers, answering what needs
them, reviewing a change, starting or stopping work.

**What the mock must show.** Below 640 px wide: one header (menu, title with place and state,
Needs you, More); a drawer with every thread and its state, Needs you first; Needs you, History
and the palette as full-screen sheets with a Close button; composer options as a bottom sheet;
the Board as cards. 44 px targets, no key legends, no horizontal scroll.

**Out of scope.** Desktop and tablet widths are unchanged. No new features: every control
already exists on desktop.
